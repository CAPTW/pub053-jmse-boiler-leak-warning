# Full Paper Table, Figure, Script, and Provenance Mapping (`PUB-053`)

This manifest maps every numbered table (**Tables 1–8**) and figure (**Figures 1–7**) in the revised submission manuscript (`JMSE-MDPI-보일러 논문 [김겸원]_인용자동화_개정본.docx` / `.pdf`) to its exact upstream origin, archived verification artifact, and release-repository script.

## 1. Execution-Layer Separation
1. **Original Simulator Execution**: The 24 paired six-hour simulation trajectories (`12` normal + `12` tube-leak episodes; `259,224` timestamps) were generated once by executing the deterministic `boiler_pdm_sim` / `ab4` closed-loop simulator and preserved in the study owner's external primary archive `observable_baseline_study_20260915T215200Z_be142b32.zip` (`data/observations/<episode>.npz`, `data/traces/<episode>.npz`, `data/block_design.json`, `data/original_configs/*.json`).
2. **R2 Reanalysis Pipeline (Stored-Data Only)**: The preserved R2 scripts in `PUB053_Manuscript_Package_R2.zip` (`code/reproduce_analysis.py`, `code/verify_features.py`, `code/test_analysis.py`, `code/make_figures_r2.py`) **did not generate new simulation trajectories or modify physical states**; they read the frozen `.npz` outputs to verify EWLS fits (`results/independent_fit.json`), recompute empirical quantile thresholds and sequential vs. interval alarms (`results/episode_method_results.csv`, `results/endpoint_sensitivity.csv`), and render the R2 plot panels.
3. **Full Paper Preparation, Citation Automation, and Release Verification**: Deterministic scripts in `manuscript_verification/` assembled the JMSE template (`build_full_paper.py`, `combine_normal_figs.py`), added structured inline Content Control citations (`<w:sdt>`) + live Word `REF` cross-references (`create_automated_citation_docx.py`, `sync_citations_and_references.py`, `word_update_export_and_test.py`), and cross-checked Tables 1–8 and Figures 1–7 against `results_archived/` (`verify_revised_manuscript.py`, `verify_tables_from_archived_results.py`, `verify_citations_and_manuscript.py`) without altering any underlying simulation numbers.
